ClipGrab

Paste a link from Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit or 1,000+ other sites, pick the moment, and download it.

It picks up the link from your current tab, and can use your login for private posts and stories.

  1. Download clipgrab-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Pick one

Seconds or m:ss, like 1:30. Leave both empty for the whole video.

The ClipGrab extension can use your login for this.
